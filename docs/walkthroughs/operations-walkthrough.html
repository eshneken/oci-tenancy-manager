    Design contract notes · candidate fields

    This walkthrough proposes separate operation state, stage and waiting reason. Candidate underlying states: queued, running, waiting, needs_attention, succeeded, cancelled, failed. They are not approved production enums. The six samples illustrate selected states, not every failure/cancellation contract. No cancellation, mutation or retry command is implemented here.

    Standalone design reference. All identities and observations are illustrative. No backend, queue, credential or provider operation is connected.
